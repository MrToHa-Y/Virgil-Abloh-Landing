"Off-white x Louis vuitton"

The Off-White x Louis Vuitton collaboration was not formal, but Virgil Abloh's influence as creative director of Louis Vuitton's men's line was felt across both brands. Abloh carried elements of Off-White's aesthetic into LV's luxury fashion, including street style, graphics, quotes and experimental materials.

"Off-White x IKEA"

The Off-White x IKEA collaboration, released in 2019 under the name Markerad, combined minimalism and street style. The collection included furniture and accessories: rugs with the inscription “KEEP OFF”, transparent bags, tables, chairs and clocks with ironic designs. The project emphasized the accessibility of art in everyday life, combining functionality and aesthetics. The collection instantly became a cult favorite among young people.

"Off-white x Nike"

The Off-White x Nike collaboration, which began in 2017 with The Ten collection, reimagined 10 iconic sneaker models including the Air Jordan 1, Air Force 1 and Nike Blazer. Virgil Abloh added signature details like quotes, visible seams, bright accents and industrial style. The collection came to symbolize a new era of street fashion, and subsequent releases such as the Dunk Low and Vapor Street reinforced the success of the partnership. These sneakers became collectors' items and changed the perception of athletic footwear in the fashion industry.

"Off-white x Mercedes-Benz"

The Off-White x Mercedes-Benz collaboration, unveiled in 2021, focused on reimagining the iconic G-Class SUV. Abloh and team created the Project Geländewagen concept model, transforming the vehicle into an art object with a minimalist design, simplified form and industrial elements. The aim was to erase the boundaries between automobile, art and fashion. The project became a symbol of innovation and emphasized Virgil Abloh's influence on design in different industries.

"Off-white x Convers"

The Off-White x Converse collaboration was part of the iconic The Ten (2017) collection and reimagined the iconic Chuck Taylor All Star. Abloh added a transparent TPU material upper, signature quotes (“VULCANIZED” on the outsole), brightly colored laces and the Off-White logo with diagonal stripes. The design combined Converse's history with modern street style. These sneakers have become a symbol of experimentation and innovation, continuing Virgil's legacy in the footwear industry.
